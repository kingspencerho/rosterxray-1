A.Read this before the draft starts

Six rules. If you remember nothing else, these prevent every big mistake.

  1. Take the best player on the page, not the position you feel like you need. Through round 7, just take whoever is highest on that round's list. Filling holes comes later.
  2. Do not draft a quarterback before round 5. Only 16 quarterbacks start in this league and about 32 get drafted. There will be a good one available late. Every year people panic and take one early, and every year it costs them a starting running back.
  3. Kicker and defense are your last two picks. Always. Rounds 14 and 15, no exceptions. They are close to interchangeable, and a kicker taken in round 11 costs you a real player.
  4. You only have 6 bench spots, so do not draft backups. No second quarterback until very late, no stashing injured players, and no handcuffs, meaning the backup to a running back you already own, drafted only in case yours gets hurt. Every bench spot must be somebody who could start for you this season. Every term in this document is defined in section B.
  5. Catches are points. One catch equals one point. A receiver with 100 catches starts the season with 100 points banked before a single yard. When two players look close, take the one who catches more passes. This is the single biggest difference between this league and a normal one.
  6. Check the injury report the morning of the draft. This sheet was verified on Aug 26, but the 53 man roster deadline is Aug 30 and players are being cut, traded and injured every day this week. Five names in wide circulation are already wrong, and they are listed in section I at the end. Two minutes of checking beats a wasted pick.
