E.The playoff weeks

Weeks 15, 16 and 17 are when the league is decided, because that is when the playoffs are played. This section rates every recommended player by how hard those three specific games look, which is called his playoff slate. Read the rule below before the tables, because it is the part people get wrong.

The rule, and it has one exception

A December schedule breaks a tie. It never makes a good player bad or a bad player good. If two players are close and you cannot decide, take the one with the better playoff slate. That is the whole use. Never pass on a clearly better player because his week 16 looks hard.

The exception, and it is why this section exists at all: in a normal 12 team league you would mostly ignore this, because if December goes wrong you just pick somebody up. In a 16 team league with six bench spots there is nobody to pick up. 240 players are already rostered and the waiver wire is empty from day one. That makes the December schedule much closer to locked at draft time than it would be anywhere else, which is why it is worth a look here and not in most leagues.

Two honest limits on this section

This is the least reliable information in the whole document, and it is not close. The ratings use what each defense gave up last season, held constant. Defenses change coordinators, lose starters and get better or worse, and none of that is in here. Treat it as a nudge, never as a fact.

This assumes the playoffs are weeks 15, 16 and 17, which is the standard Yahoo setup. Confirm that in the league settings before leaning on any of it. If the league runs weeks 14 to 16 instead, this whole section points at the wrong games.
