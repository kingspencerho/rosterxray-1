I.If you remember five things

  1. One catch equals one point, so take the player who catches more. Most rankings online assume half a point. They are quietly wrong for this league, and the gap is largest at running back.
  2. Quarterback in round 5 or later, never earlier. Mahomes was available in round 6 and Kyler Murray in round 9. That is what waiting buys you.
  3. Tight end is either round 3 or round 5, not in between. Take McBride or Bowers early, or wait for Fannin, Warren or Pitts. The middle is where you overpay.
  4. Kicker and defense with your last two picks. Nothing else. This is the most common way beginners lose value and it is completely avoidable.
  5. Check the roster before every pick, because other people's lists are wrong right now. Ricky Pearsall and Jayden Higgins are out for the 2026 season. Trey Benson was released by Arizona. James Conner is third string. Kayshon Boutte plays for Houston now, not New England. All five are still sitting at normal prices on lists that have not been updated. Where these numbers come from

Draft prices are Yahoo redraft average draft position, refreshed Aug 15, 2026 from 2,429 real drafts held between Aug 10 and Aug 15. Prices move, so treat anything within a round as noise rather than a precise ranking.

Roles, injuries, trades and depth charts were re-verified against live reporting on Aug 26, 2026. Every entry marked "Updated Aug 26" or "Added Aug 26" reflects that pass. Two players were removed outright: James Conner, who is third on Arizona's depth chart, and Trey Benson, who Arizona released.

Catch totals, target shares and weekly consistency are measured from the 2025 season. A player's 2025 numbers describe what happened, not a guarantee of what will happen, and rookies have no 2025 numbers at all, so their entries lean on reported camp and preseason usage instead.

⚠️ The 53 man roster deadline is Aug 30, 2026 at 6pm Eastern, and cuts are happening right now. Depth charts will move between this document and any draft held after that date. Check the injury report and your league's player list on the morning of the draft. That is not a formality this week, it is the single highest value thing you can do.

One honest caveat about the prices. The source market is half point per catch, and this league is full point. That difference is deliberate rather than an error, and it is exactly where the value comes from: players who catch a lot of passes are priced below what they are worth here. The ones marked PPR bonus are where that gap is largest.
