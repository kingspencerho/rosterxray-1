Careful in round 1

Ashton Jeanty (LV) has a sprained ankle, confirmed Aug 25 as not long term, but his Week 1 availability is genuinely uncertain. Ankle sprains take one to six weeks. Fine to draft, but know you may be starting the season without him.

Christian McCaffrey (SF) caught 102 passes and had zero bad games, but his injury history is a real risk rather than a small one. Fine if you accept that trade.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba (SEA) had a huge year built on deep throws that do not repeat reliably. Priced like a top five pick, riskier than that price suggests.

Rashee Rice (KC) is coming back from a knee injury and played only 8 games. Reports were positive in early August, but this is a bet, not a safe pick.

2Take the other onePicks 17–32

If you took a running back in round 1, take a receiver here, or the other way round. Still no quarterback, still no tight end unless it is one of the two in round 3.

RB
Omarion HamptonLACADP 18.7

Second year back with real evidence behind the hype: four games as a rookie above 75% of snaps, and over 80% of his team's carries inside the 5 yard line when he was on the field. That is where touchdowns come from.

RB
Derrick HenryBALADP 19.1

Has beaten the age question every year so far. Baltimore runs the ball a lot and he is the goal line back, which is the touchdown job.

RB
Kenneth WalkerKCADP 20.3

Signed a large contract in Kansas City, which is the clearest signal a team can send about who gets the ball. Mahomes returning adds passing game value on top.

WR
Drake LondonATLADP 17.0

RB
Jeremiyah LoveARIADP 26.6

Rookie who started the preseason opener with the first team, took about 75% of the snaps over the first three drives, and got all 14 first half touches. Teams do not do that by accident.
Updated Aug 26, and this changes the pick: he suffered a high ankle sprain in that same game, will not play again in the preseason, and Arizona is hoping to have him back for Week 1 roughly four weeks out. High ankle sprains are slow and they linger. He is no longer a round 2 pick. If he falls several rounds he is worth taking, but do not spend a top 30 selection on a rookie who has not practiced.

Avoid in round 2

Josh Jacobs (GB) has a legal situation from June 2026 that creates real risk he misses games. Not worth it at this price.

Kyren Williams (LAR) is the starter but not a true every down back, and the price assumes he is.
