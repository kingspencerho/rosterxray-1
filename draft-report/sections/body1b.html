C.The draft plan, round by round

What to be looking for at each stage, and the one rule that governs it. Follow this and you cannot build a broken roster.

RdTakeThe rule for this stage
1–2RB or WR
best available		 Do not think about need yet. Take the highest player on the list, twice. Aim to come out with one running back and one receiver, but if the board gives you two of the same, take them. No quarterback, no tight end, no kicker.
3TE fork
or RB / WR		 The only round where a tight end is worth this price. If McBride or Bowers is there, take him and the position is solved for the season. If both are gone, take a running back or receiver and do not touch tight end again until round 5.
4RB or WR
fill the gap		 Now look at what you have. Four lead running backs sit in this round at half the price of round 2. If you went receiver twice early, this is the catch-up round.
5–6QB opens
+ TE fallback		 First reasonable place for a quarterback. Hurts and Daniels in 5, Mahomes in 6. If you still have no tight end, Fannin, Warren or Pitts in round 5 is your spot. Do not leave round 6 without a plan for both.
7Last starter
flex spot		 By the end of this round your nine starting spots should be filled except kicker and defense. Everything from here is bench.
8–10Bench who could start Three picks. Each one must be somebody you would be willing to put in your lineup if a starter got hurt. No handcuffs, no second quarterback, no injured stashes. Six bench spots is too few for luxuries.
11–13Sleepers
upside only		 The goal changes here. Stop drafting proven-but-limited players and start drafting players whose job could get bigger. Section D is the list. Every name there has a specific stated reason, not a hunch.
14Defense Pick any defense playing a weak offense in week 1. They are close to interchangeable and you can change them week to week.
15Kicker Any kicker on a good offense. This is the most replaceable position in fantasy football and it goes last, every time.

Check your roster at these three points

After round 4 4 of 7

You should have four of your seven skill starters, weighted toward running back and receiver. If you have three receivers and no running back, take backs in rounds 5 and 6 and do not wait.

After round 7 7 of 7

Quarterback, two running backs, two receivers, tight end and flex all filled. If a spot is still empty here, fill it with your next pick regardless of who else is on the board.

After round 13 13 players

Seven starters plus six bench. Two picks left, and they are the defense and the kicker. Nothing else.

If the draft goes sideways

Two things go wrong in every draft. Here is what to do about each.

  1. A run happens on a position and you panic. Somebody takes a quarterback in round 4, then four more go in the next ten picks. Do not join in. There are 32 quarterbacks drafted for 16 starting spots, so the position cannot run out. Kyler Murray was named a week 1 starter and goes in round 9. Let other people spend early picks there.
  2. Your position is empty and the good ones are gone. This only really happens at tight end. If you get to round 8 without one, take Kittle or Kelce there. Both caught more than 55 passes last year and both are sitting at bench prices because of age or missed time. There is no scenario where you need to reach.
