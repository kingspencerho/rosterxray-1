F.Three things that make this league different

League size 16 teams

240 players get drafted. In a normal 12-team league that number is 180. Sixty extra players come off the board, so the waiver wire will be empty from day one. There is no fixing a bad draft here.

Scoring Full PPR

One point per catch, not a half point. Pass catching running backs and high volume receivers are worth more here than any ranking list you find online, because most lists assume half.

Bench 6 spots

Nine starters and only six on the bench. That is very tight. Every bench pick should be a player who could realistically start for you, not a lottery ticket.
