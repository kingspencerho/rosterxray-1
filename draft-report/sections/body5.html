WR

Traded Aug 24. He is no longer on New England, he is a Houston Texan. Most lists have not updated this. The trade happened specifically because Houston lost Jayden Higgins for the season, so Boutte walks into a vacated starting job opposite Nico Collins rather than the crowded room he left. He caught 33 passes for 551 yards and 6 touchdowns last year and ranked fifth in the NFL in yards per catch.
