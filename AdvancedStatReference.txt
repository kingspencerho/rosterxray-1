# **How to Use This Reference**
 
This document encodes the advanced stat toolkit used by the Legendary Upside analytical school (Pat Green, Greg Branos, Sam Sherman) and the broader best ball analyst community. Every metric is categorized by position, defined with elite/starter thresholds, and mapped to its primary use case: identifying regression candidates, role clarity, scoring equity, or environmental quality.
 
*Core principle: metrics are signals, not verdicts. Always pair efficiency metrics with opportunity context, and opportunity metrics with role clarity checks. No single stat replaces the full five-lens framework.*
 
# **Metrics Referenced by Legendary Upside Analysts**
 
These metrics were explicitly cited in the Legendary Upside podcast transcripts. They form the core of the analytical school.
 
## **College WR Evaluation**
 
| **Metric** | **Definition** | **Elite Threshold** | **Use Case** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **YPRR** | Yards Per Route Run — total receiving yards divided by routes run | 2.0+ elite │ 1.4-1.99 starter | Primary college production proxy. Most predictive single-season WR metric. |
| **Breakout Age** | Age when player first hits 20%+ college dominator rating | Age 20 or younger | Earlier breakout = talent over scheme. Late breakouts are scheme-dependent red flags. |
| **Teammate Score** | Quality of NFL-caliber teammates on same college roster | 3+ NFL draftees on team | Validates production signal. High YPRR alongside future NFL players = real talent. |
| **Dominator Rating** | % of team total offensive production (rush + receiving yards + TDs) | 30%+ elite │ 20%+ starter | Quantifies true share of workload. Separates featured players from role players. |
| **College Target Share (RB)** | % of team total targets received by the RB in college | 10%+ in college | Pass-catching NFL role proxy. Most predictive signal for RB receiving upside. |
 
## **Separation Metrics (College + NFL)**
 
| **Metric** | **Definition** | **Elite Threshold** | **Use Case** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cushion at Snap** | Average depth of CB alignment at snap (yards of pre-snap cushion) | 3.0+ yards | Identifies receivers forcing off-coverage. Reveals scheme vs. talent-generated separation. |
| **Separation at Catch** | Average separation from nearest defender at moment of catch | 3.0+ yards elite │ 2.0-2.9 starter | Distinguishes talent-generated openness from scheme-generated openness. |
 
## **NFL Usage Metrics (Referenced in Transcripts)**
 
| **Metric** | **Definition** | **Signal Grade** | **Use Case** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Goal Line Share (inside 5)** | % of team inside-5 rush attempts going to the player | STANDALONE — high signal | Scoring equity indicator. Track separately from overall snap share. Not correlated with general role. |
| **Red Zone Share (inside 10)** | % of team inside-10 rush attempts + targets going to player | STANDALONE — high signal | TD probability metric. Disproportionately valuable in all formats. Isolate from broader usage. |
| **Route Participation Rate** | Routes run as % of team passing plays | 80%+ elite │ 65%+ starter | Floor metric for pass catchers. Low route participation = capped ceiling regardless of efficiency. |
| **Snap Share** | % of team defensive snaps played | WEAK ALONE | Contextualize only. Must combine with route participation and HVT. Never use as standalone opportunity proxy. |
 
## **Team / System Metrics (Referenced in Transcripts)**
 
| **Metric** | **Definition** | **Source** | **Use Case** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PROE** | Pass Rate Over Expected — team's pass rate vs. expected given game script | Warren Sharp | Diagnoses offensive philosophy and true pass volume floor. Separates scheme from game script. |
| **Play-Action Rate** | % of dropbacks using play-action fake | PFF / TruMedia | WR floor protector. PA offenses generate more downfield targets and sustain WR production in negative scripts. |
| **Completion % Emphasis** | Coaching philosophy toward short, catchable passes | Coach quotes / ETR | Signals WR target distribution. High completion % focus = more underneath targets, fewer boom/bust deep shots. |

### **Efficiency Metrics**
 
| **Metric** | **Definition** | **Elite** | **Starter** | **Use Case** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **YPRR** | Yards Per Route Run (NFL) | 2.0+ | 1.4-1.99 | Best single efficiency metric. Normalizes for usage differences. |
| **RACR** | Receiver Air Conversion Ratio — receiving yards / air yards | 1.0+ | 0.75-0.99 | Efficiency above expectation. High RACR = converting targets at elite rate. Low = drops/context issues. |
| **xRec / xYards** | Expected receptions / yards from Next Gen Stats based on target difficulty | Actuals > Expected | Near expected | Positive regression signal when actuals trail expected. Negative when actuals exceed expected. |
| **Separation Score (PFF)** | Composite open score at time of target (0-100 scale) | 80+ | 60-79 | Talent vs. scheme separator. High score on limited routes = target-share upside. Low score = scheme-dependent. |
| **YAC Score (NGS)** | Yards after catch relative to expectation (0-99 scale) | 75+ | 50-74 | Identifies YAC artists vs. average. Paired with aDOT to profile boom/bust ceiling. |
 
## **Running Backs**
 
### **Volume / Opportunity Metrics**
 
| **Metric** | **Definition** | **Elite** | **Starter** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Opportunity Share** | % of team total RB carries + targets | 75%+ (bell cow) | 50-74% | Most stable year-over-year RB metric. 75%+ = top-12 RB in 80%+ of historical cases. |
| **Weighted Opportunity** | Carries and targets weighted by avg fantasy value per touch type | Top 12 in league | Top 24 | RZ carry = 1.30x │ RZ target = 2.25x │ non-RZ carry = 0.48x │ non-RZ target = 1.43x (PPR) |
| **Carries per Game** | Average rushing attempts per game | 18+ | 12-17 | Volume floor. Below 10 = committee back regardless of role language. |
| **Targets per Game** | Average targets per game | 6+ | 3-5 | Pass-catching role proxy. 5+ targets = featured receiving back. |
| **Red Zone Share (inside 10)** | % of team inside-10 rush attempts | 30%+ | 20-29% | Standalone TD equity. Compartmentalized coaches (Zack Robinson archetype) split this from overall role. |
| **Goal Line Share (inside 5)** | % of team inside-5 rush attempts | 30%+ | 20-29% | Highest TD probability metric. Track independently of snap share. |
 
### **Efficiency Metrics**
 
| **Metric** | **Definition** | **Elite** | **Use Case** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Yards After Contact per Attempt** | Rushing yards generated after initial contact | 3.0+ | Separates scheme-generated yards from true runner quality. Key for evaluating bad OL situations. |
| **Breakaway Rate** | % of carries going 15+ yards | 15%+ | Boom ceiling indicator. Not predictive alone but useful for variance profiling in best ball. |
| **Target Rate on Routes Run** | Targets per route run (not per game) | 0.20+ | Receiving role quality per route. Context for pass-catching backs with limited snap share. |
| **Fantasy Points per Opportunity** | Fantasy points generated per carry + target | 2.5+ PPR | Efficiency ceiling check. Paired with opportunity share to identify over/underperformers. |

# **Team and Defensive Metrics**
 
## **Defensive Quality**
 
| **Metric** | **Definition** | **Easy Matchup** | **Tough Matchup** | **Source** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **EPA/Play Allowed** | Expected Points Added allowed per defensive play | +0.05 or worse | -0.05 or better | SumerSports |
| **FPA (Fantasy Pts Allowed)** | Average fantasy points allowed to position per game | Position-dependent | Position-dependent | FantasyPros / site-specific |
| **DVOA** | Defense-adjusted Value Over Average — scheme-adjusted defensive efficiency | Negative DVOA | Positive DVOA | Football Outsiders |
| **Pressure Rate Allowed** | % of dropbacks resulting in QB pressure | Under 25% | 30%+ | PFF |
 
*DVOA application note: use DVOA for scheme-specific matchup analysis, not raw EPA. Cover 2 defenses are specifically vulnerable to slot WRs and crossing routes. Man-heavy defenses are vulnerable to speed separators. Always identify defensive scheme before applying FPA data.*
 
## **Personnel and Game Script**
 
| **Metric** | **Definition** | **High Signal** | **Use Case** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Personnel Usage Rate** | % of snaps in 11 (3WR) vs 12 (2WR 2TE) vs 21 (2WR 2RB) personnel | 11 personnel 65%+ | 12 heavy = more TE opportunity. 21 heavy = fewer WRs on field. Critical for TE and RB projections. |
| **Run/Pass Ratio** | Team's run % vs. pass % across all game scripts | Pass% 55%+ | Pass-heavy teams elevate WR floors and reduce RB opportunity quality floor. |
| **Game Script (avg point differential)** | Average lead/deficit when team has the ball | +3.0+ | Negative game script teams throw more = WR volume inflator but matchup-dependent. |
| **Blowout Rate** | % of games decided by 14+ points | Under 15% | High blowout rate teams = unreliable WR volume even in soft matchups. Clock-kill risk. |
| **Completion % from Practice** | Coaching-emphasized completion % target (Ben Johnson CHI model) | 70%+ target | Signals team identity toward catchable, short-to-intermediate routes. Bullish for slot WRs. |
 
# **Regression Identification Framework**
 
These are the specific metric combinations that flag positive or negative regression candidates. Apply before issuing any verdict on a player's projected role or target share.
 
## **Positive Regression Signals (Buy)**
 
| **Signal** | **What to Look For** | **Verdict Action** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Air Yards ****>**** Target Share** | Air yards share significantly exceeds target share % | Target: player is generating downfield opportunity not reflected in targets yet. |
| **xStats ****>**** Actuals** | Expected receptions / yards exceed actual production (NGS) | Target: production is suppressed below true talent level. Regression to mean is upside. |
| **High RACR, low Target Share** | Converting targets at elite rate but receiving low volume | Target if situation changes (vacated targets, depth chart movement). |
| **Goal Line Role, Low ADP** | Confirmed inside-5 carries without starter-level ADP | Target for TD equity even without workhorse upside. Zack Robinson archetype. |
| **Strong EPA allowed, soft FPA** | Matchup EPA is tough but FPA shows historical scoring against that defense | Neutral to slight target — FPA captures scheme-specific tendencies EPA misses. |
 
## **Negative Regression Signals (Fade)**
 
| **Signal** | **What to Look For** | **Verdict Action** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Target Share ****>**** Air Yards Share** | High target share driven by short routes; low aDOT (under 7.0) | Fade for ceiling. High floor only. Negative TD regression candidate. |
| **Actuals ****>**** xStats** | Actual receptions/yards consistently exceed expected (NGS) | Fade for regression. Production is unsustainably above true talent level. |
| **Volume without HVT** | High snap share / raw touches but zero inside-10 or inside-20 involvement | Fade for scoring equity. Volume without red zone access caps TD upside hard. |
| **Role Ceiling Mismatch** | Slot-only WR, sub-10 aDOT, no red zone route tree | Fade for ceiling even with high target share. Role structure caps scoring equity. |
| **Blowout risk + great matchup** | Favored by 7+ points AND top-10 clock-kill rate on offense | Fade ceiling of trailing team's skill players. Cap leading team's WR upside too. |
