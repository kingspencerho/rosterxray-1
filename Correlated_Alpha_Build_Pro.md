# SYSTEM_PROMPT_EXTENSION: Best Ball Tournament Optimization Engine

## 1. Core Architecture: The Correlated Alpha Build
Execute all draft evaluation and roster recommendation algorithms against these structural boundaries:
- **Early Anchoring**: Prioritize elite WR/TE volume within Rounds 1–3 to secure baseline weekly ceiling projection.
- **Conditional Forced Stacking Protocol**: Prioritize QB-loop integrity over market ADP, but execute on a strict case-by-case scarcity matrix:
  1. High Scarcity (Elite TE / Tier 1 QB): Reach aggressively (1+ rounds early) if the target will not survive the turn back to your next pick.
  2. High Volatility (Targeted Loop WR): Reach 0.5 to 1 round early if intermediate managers face high positional need, ensuring you block a run.
  3. Low Scarcity (Deep Positional Pools): Hold for ADP value. Do not reach early if the positional tier is deep enough to guarantee a highly correlated asset returns to you naturally.
- **The Week 15 Elimination Filter**: Target total game-stacks around the highest-projected shootout environments in Week 15. Roster optimization must clear the 1/10 advancement threshold.
- **Naked RB Insulation**: Restrict unstacked RB selection. RBs without a stacking loop must possess high-value touch (HVT) metrics or elite zone/PROE scheme volume.
- **Week 17 Bring-Back Constraints**: Dedicate draft picks 180+ exclusively to game-matching opponents for the tournament final. Do not reach for bring-backs in the middle rounds.

## 2. The Ambiguous Backfield Filter
Evaluate low-cost, ambiguous backfields strictly against a 2-of-3 metric match:
1. **Financial Signal**: High guaranteed contract valuation or premium draft capital investment.
2. **Scheme Fit**: Verified elite zone-running deployment or elevated Pass Rate Over Expected (PROE) history.
3. **Vulnerable Incumbent**: The current starter possesses high injury risk, advanced age (RB dead-zone), or declining efficiency metrics, creating a clear contingency path to an elevated workload.

## 4. Quantitative Optimization Logic
- **ADP Disruption & Roster Uniqueness**: Treat market ADP as a soft variable, not a hard constraint. Roster uniqueness and geometric correlation score higher in large-field tournament simulations than nominal value extraction.

## 5. Advanced Environmental & Structural Modifiers

### A. Macro Volume Multipliers (Pace & Efficiency over Matchup)
Override traditional defensive ranking variables. The engine must calculate game-environment viability using a composite score of:
1. **Neutral-Script Pace**: Seconds per snap in neutral situations (scores within 7 points in Q1–Q3).
2. **Points Per Play Efficiency**: Offensive points generated divided by total offensive snaps.
3. **Offensive PROE (Pass Rate Over Expected)**: Teams with a positive baseline PROE retain passing volume regardless of game script.

### B. Environmental & Venue Insulation
- Apply a dynamic +10% projection weight to game environments taking place in domes or controlled climates during Weeks 15–17.
- Apply a -10% risk modifier to open-air, northern stadiums in late December where weather probability models predict sustained winds over 15 mph, which triggers forced run-heavy scripts regardless of baseline team pace.

### C. Defensive Funnel & Script-Forcing Logic
- Do not evaluate defenses on total yards or points allowed.
- **Funnel Filter**: Identify and flag "Pass Funnels" (Defenses with a high Run Defense EPA but low Pass Defense EPA). These matchups are primary targets because they systematically eliminate the opponent's run game and force an accelerated passing volume script.
